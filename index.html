Statement

Business lineInvoicedCollectedDeposits keptStill owedSpentNet

Each invoice is split across business lines by its items (set on the price list), so ice sold on a rental invoice counts under Ice. Discounts come off Rentals unless the invoice has no rental items. The delivery charge on each invoice shows as its own Delivery row. Deposits count under the invoice's own line. “Spent” is expenses dated in the period (General = shared costs). “Net” is money collected minus money spent. “Invoiced” counts invoices dated in the period (not quotes, not cancelled; deposits excluded). “Collected” counts payments received in the period, deposits included. “Still owed” is today's unpaid balance on that period's invoices.

Price list

Items you rent or sell often. Rate is per item (per day for rentals). Put in Stock owned for rental items and the app warns you when two bookings on the same dates need more than you have. Leave it blank for things like ice. Untick Per day for things sold once (ice, delivery): their Days column shows “—”. Replacement cost is charged when an item comes back missing or damaged. Business line is where the item's money counts in Reports — so ice on a rental invoice counts as Ice.
